A practical support toolkit

Help that leaves room for dignity.

You cannot make someone change, but you can create safer conditions for honesty, accountability, and the next good decision.

Start the conversation

Use calm, open language that makes it easier to talk about what is really happening.

Agree on boundaries

Talk together about practical limits, access, money, and what support feels useful.

Connect the tools

BetControl can help the person you support block distractions and build accountability on their own terms.

A GENTLER OPENING
“I care about you, and I want to understand what this has been like.”

Lead with what you have noticed, ask what support would feel helpful, and give the conversation time.

Before you step in

Support starts with a conversation, not a confrontation.

There is no perfect script. A calm, specific, non-judgemental opening can make it easier for someone to be honest and hear you.

Choose a private moment without a crisis happening around you.
Describe what you have noticed instead of labelling the person.
Ask what kind of help they are ready to consider.
Set boundaries that protect your household and your wellbeing.
Keep it sustainable

Three steps for showing up with care.

01 / NOTICE

Name what you see

Focus on specific changes and how they affect the person, the family, or the household.

02 / TALK

Make space to listen

Ask open questions and let the person describe their experience in their own words.

03 / SUPPORT

Agree on the next step

Choose one practical action, such as exploring BetControl or finding additional support.

You do not have to carry the whole thing alone.
