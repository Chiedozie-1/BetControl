A practical support toolkit

Be there without carrying everything.

You cannot change someone else’s choices, but you can make space for honesty, protect your own wellbeing, and point toward useful support.

Open the door

Use calm, specific language that makes it safer to talk about what is happening.

Protect your peace

Set clear limits around money, trust, and what you can realistically offer.

Offer a next step

Invite them to explore tools like BetControl when they are ready to build healthier boundaries.

Healthy boundaries

Care does not mean doing it all for them.

Support is more sustainable when it includes your own limits. Be honest about what you can give, what you cannot fund, and what you need to feel safe.

Talk about the specific behavior you have noticed, without labels.
Decide what financial and emotional boundaries you need.
Ask what support they are ready to consider today.
Keep your own support network close.
A GENTLER OPENING
“I care about you, and I also need us to talk about what this is costing.”

Honesty and compassion can sit in the same conversation. Start with what is true for you.

Keep it sustainable

Three ways to show up with care.

01 / NOTICE

Name what you see

Focus on clear changes and how they are affecting the person and your relationship.

02 / TALK

Make space to listen

Ask open questions, then let the person speak without rushing to solve everything.

03 / SUPPORT

Choose one next step

Agree on something practical, from exploring BetControl to finding professional help.

You can be part of the way forward.

Start with one honest conversation and one boundary that protects both of you.
